Our Story

About STHR

Sell The Heart Records is an independent punk and indie rock label that specializes in not specializing in any specific genre.

From our humble beginnings Santa Rosa, CA to our current home in the East Bay Area, Sell The Heart Records has always strived to keep our catalog eclectic and fun.

With over 100 releases to our name, we have released albums from bands all across the United States, Canada, the U.K., the E.U., and even South Korea

We pride ourselves for having a variety of styles to offer, everything from punk to ska to post-rock to queercore to post-punk and more.
