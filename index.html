Our Story

About STHR

Sell The Heart Records is an independent punk and indie rock label with a passion for releasing music that matters — music made with heart, honesty, and something to say.

Since our founding, we've worked with bands across the United States and beyond, pressing vinyl and CDs with care, and getting records into the hands of people who love music as much as the artists who made it.

We believe in supporting scenes, not just bands. That means working with local record stores, booking stages at fests, and building community around the music we love.

Every release on our label is something we believe in — not just a product, but a document of a moment in music worth preserving.
